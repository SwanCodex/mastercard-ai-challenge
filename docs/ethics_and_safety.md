# SENTINEL — Ethics & Safety

## Purpose

SENTINEL is a security research prototype for evaluating and defending AI-driven payment workflows against adversarial attacks.

The project is intended for controlled experimentation, benchmarking, and demonstration purposes only.

## Data Policy

- Use public benchmark datasets such as IEEE-CIS Fraud Detection and PaySim.
- Do not use real Mastercard customer data.
- Do not use real payment-card numbers, bank-account numbers, credentials, or personally identifiable financial information.
- Synthetic transaction and identity data must be clearly separated from real-world data.

## Voice and Audio Safety

- Voice-cloning experiments may use only recordings of team members who have explicitly consented.
- Do not clone, imitate, or generate the voice of a person without their permission.
- Generated audio must remain within the project environment and must not be used for impersonation outside the controlled demonstration.
- Do not target real individuals, financial institutions, customers, or employees.

## Synthetic Identity Safety

- Synthetic identities are simulated research artifacts.
- Generated identities must not correspond to real people.
- Do not use real identity documents, government identifiers, payment credentials, or stolen personal information.
- Synthetic account-aging experiments must remain entirely within the project's simulated environment.

## Agentic Payment Attack Safety

- Payment agents are mock/simulated agents.
- Merchant pages, invoices, tool outputs, and counterparties used in attacks must be synthetic or controlled by the team.
- No attacks may be directed against real payment systems, merchants, banks, websites, APIs, or AI agents.
- Attack payloads are intended for evaluation of the project's own defensive system.

## Responsible Disclosure

If testing reveals a vulnerability in an external system or third-party software, testing will stop and the issue will not be exploited further.

Any potentially reportable vulnerability will be documented and handled through the appropriate responsible-disclosure process.

## Project Scope

SENTINEL is a prototype and is not intended to authorize, execute, or interfere with real financial transactions.

All demonstrations should use synthetic transactions and controlled environments.

## Team Agreement

Before beginning voice-cloning, synthetic-identity, or adversarial payment experiments, all three team members must acknowledge these safety boundaries.
